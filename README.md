# SME EU AI Act Compliance Checklist

A free, self-serve web checklist that walks non-technical SME business owners
through what EU AI Act Article 50 requires for AI-generated content on their
website, and flags where they need — or don't need — hands-on help to fix it.

**Status: in development, shipping in two phases.** The marketing campaign
this product supports launches **Friday 11 September 2026**. There isn't
enough runway to build the full checklist by then, so a placeholder
"coming soon" landing page ships for that date instead (Phase 0); the full
interactive checklist follows as a fast-follow (Phase 1), targeted to land
well before the Article 50 marking grace period closes in December 2026. No
checklist content, design, or code exists yet — this repo currently holds
planning documents (`docs/opportunity-brief.md`, `docs/prd.md`).

## Who it's for

Non-technical SME business owners who are existing clients of our digital
agency and have AI-generated content on their website. They know a compliance
deadline exists but don't have the time or legal background to figure out
what it requires of them.

## What it does

**Phase 0 (launches 11 September 2026):** a placeholder landing page that
explains what's coming, captures interest via email, and keeps campaign
traffic tracking intact — nothing interactive yet.

**Phase 1 (fast-follow, date not yet fixed):**

- Planned: Walks the owner through a plain-language checklist of what Article
  50 requires for AI-generated website content.
- Planned: Tells the owner, item by item, whether they're compliant, not
  compliant, or need to check further.
- Planned: Surfaces which items the owner can likely fix themselves versus
  which need the agency's help, with a low-friction way to ask for help if
  self-serve guidance isn't enough.
- Planned: Hands off to a paid implementation engagement with the agency for
  items the owner can't or doesn't want to self-serve.

Any copy about fines or penalties is required to caveat that SME-specific
reduced caps may apply and are not guaranteed to match a general headline
figure — see `docs/prd.md` for the exact requirement.
