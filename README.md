# EU AI Act Website Compliance Checklist

A free tool that helps small and medium business owners figure out whether
the AI-generated content on their website meets the EU AI Act's disclosure
requirements — in plain language, with no legal background required.

**Status: launching in two parts.** Part one is live now: a short page
where you can register your interest and be notified the moment the full
checklist is ready. The full interactive checklist is being built as a
fast-follow.

## Who it's for

Business owners who aren't technical or legal specialists, who know their
website has AI-generated content (text, images, or chat), and want a
straight answer about what the law requires of them — without reading the
regulation themselves.

## What it does today

The page live at **https://compliance.gro-better.com/** currently:

- Explains, briefly, why Article 50 of the EU AI Act matters if your
  website has AI-generated content.
- Lets you leave your email address so you're notified the moment the full
  checklist is ready — no account or password needed.
- Shows a cookie-consent banner before any analytics cookie is set, and
  links to a [Privacy Policy](https://compliance.gro-better.com/privacy-policy).

It does **not** yet tell you your compliance status, walk you through any
checklist items, or estimate any fine or penalty amount — that's the next
part, described below.

## What's coming next

The full checklist will:

- Walk you through what Article 50 requires for AI-generated content on
  your website, item by item, in plain language.
- Tell you whether each item looks compliant, not compliant, or needs a
  closer look.
- Give you clear instructions for items you can likely fix yourself.
- Give you a straightforward way to ask for hands-on help with anything
  you're stuck on.

It will **not** cover the rest of the EU AI Act (only Article 50's website
disclosure rules), and it will not calculate or state a specific fine
amount for your business — any mention of penalties will stay general and
caveated, since exact exposure depends on facts this tool doesn't have.

## How to use it

There's nothing to install. Visit **https://compliance.gro-better.com/** in
your browser, enter your email address, and submit the form to be notified
when the full checklist launches. That's the entire interaction today.

## Known limitations right now

- **On some older or smaller phone screens, the cookie banner can visually
  sit on top of the email field and signup button when you first load the
  page**, before you've tapped Accept or Decline. If the signup form looks
  like it's missing when you first open the page on your phone, tap either
  button on the cookie banner (or just scroll down) — the form will appear
  and work normally. This mainly affects the iPhone 6/6s/7/8/SE (2020/2022)
  screen size and some smaller/older Android phones; current-generation
  phones aren't affected. This is a known issue and a fix is planned.
- **The Privacy Policy is an early version**, published to be accurate
  about what data is and isn't collected, but it doesn't yet include the
  full legal detail (such as a formal data-controller address, stated
  retention periods, or a full rights list) that a final version will
  have. A complete legal review is planned.
- Submitting your email more than once is fine — you won't get duplicate
  confirmations or be added twice.
- The full checklist isn't available yet; submitting your email only adds
  you to the notification list for it.
