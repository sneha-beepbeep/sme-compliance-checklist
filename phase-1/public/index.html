EU AI Act website compliance checklist

This checklist covers Article 50 transparency obligations for AI-generated content on your website only — it does not cover general AI Act compliance, high-risk system classification, or GPAI provider obligations.

It works by asking you a few short, factual questions about what you've actually done on your site for each item — you don't have to self-assess directly. Based on your answers, each item is flagged as Compliant, Not compliant, or Needs-check.

No account or login is needed, and this doesn't give you legal certification — it flags where you may want to take a closer look and points you to the relevant requirement, not a fix-it guide. Takes about 5 minutes.

Non-compliance with Article 50 can carry real enforcement consequences, including SME-specific reduced penalty caps under Article 99(6) of the AI Act — this checklist doesn't calculate or estimate your individual exposure, and nothing here is legal advice.
