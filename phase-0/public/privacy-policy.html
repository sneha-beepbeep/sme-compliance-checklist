gro compliance Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 September 2026

This privacy policy ("policy") explains how gro compliance ("us", "we", "our") uses and protects the data you provide when you visit and use this service at https://compliance.gro-better.com/ ("service").

We reserve the right to change this policy at any time. If you want to stay up to date with the latest changes, we advise you to check this page periodically.

What User Data We Collect

When you use the service, we may collect the following data:

  • Your email address, if you submit it via our signup form.
  • Basic usage/analytics data about how you interact with the service, if you consent to analytics cookies.

Why We Collect Your Data

We collect your data for the following reasons:

  • To follow up with you about the EU AI Act compliance checklist you signed up for.
  • To better understand your needs and improve our service.

Safeguarding and Securing the Data

gro compliance is committed to securing your data and keeping it confidential. We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to prevent unauthorized access, disclosure, or loss of the data we collect.

Our Cookie Policy

If you accept our cookie banner, we use analytics cookies to understand how visitors use the service. This data is used in aggregate to improve the service.

Cookies used for analytics only load if you accept them; declining means no analytics cookies are set. You can also disable cookies at any time via your browser's settings.

Restricting the Collection of Your Personal Data

You can restrict how we use your data by:

  • Not submitting the signup form if you don't want to share your email address.
  • Declining the cookie consent banner if you don't want analytics cookies set.
  • Contacting us at info@gro-better.com at any time to ask us to change or delete data you've already shared.

gro compliance will not lease, sell, or distribute your personal information to any third party without your permission. Your data is stored within the EU.
